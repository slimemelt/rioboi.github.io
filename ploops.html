Ploops

tag, but you only get arms

  • VR — left stick to walk, hold left trigger to sprint, A to jump, right stick to turn. X opens your watch: name, color, room, mode, weapons and sound.
  • Desktop — WASD to move, mouse to look, space to jump, M to switch between ploops and brawl, Esc to pause.
  • Hands — grip to grab a Ploop. Pull it apart with both hands to tear it open, hold it to your mouth to eat it, pull the trigger to squish it, or throw it at something. Desktop: E grab/throw, R rip, F bite, Q squish.
  • Blaster — right trigger in VR, click on desktop. Ploops pop. They come back.
  • Chainsaw — press B in VR to cycle blaster, chainsaw and knives. Rev the chainsaw with the trigger; for knives, squeeze the trigger and let go mid-swing to throw. Desktop: 1 blaster, 2 chainsaw, 3 knives.
  • Brawl — switch modes on the watch. Everyone goes to the boxing ring: punch people hard enough to knock them out of the ring. Desktop: tick brawl mode in this menu or press M, then click to punch.
