Ploops

tag, but you only get arms

  • VR — grab the ground, walls and trees and push off to move. Swing hard and let go to leap. Right stick snap-turns, left stick up/down moves the ground closer or further.
  • Desktop — WASD to move, mouse to look, space to jump, Esc to pause.
  • Red Ploop is it. If that's you, touch someone. If not, run.
  • Blaster — right trigger in VR, click on desktop. Ploops pop. They come back.
